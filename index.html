About.

I’ve spent more than 25 years building companies, breaking systems, and leading security teams at Amazon, Cimpress, Rapid7, and ZeroFOX.

At Gomboc AI, I’m focused on the gap between finding a security problem and fixing it. We use deterministic AI to give engineering teams fixes they can actually apply.
