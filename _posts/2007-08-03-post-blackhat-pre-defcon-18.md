So it's been a really hectic couple of days here in Vegas. We are here (myself and 2 members of MCRC - Aviv &amp; Amir), running between presentations, and handling booth/media traffic.

The really interesting trend here is the amount of research that touched web security (in the sense that we at Finjan are focused on). From <a href="http://www.blackhat.com/html/bh-usa-07/bh-usa-07-speakers.html#Kaminsky" target="_blank">Kaminsky</a> who talked about the browser being the attack vector to get to the core of the intranet, through the <a href="http://www.blackhat.com/html/bh-usa-07/bh-usa-07-speakers.html#Sullivan" target="_blank">research</a> <a href="http://www.blackhat.com/html/bh-usa-07/bh-usa-07-speakers.html#Hoffman" target="_blank">from</a> SPI (kudos Billy), <a href="http://www.blackhat.com/html/bh-usa-07/bh-usa-07-speakers.html#Feinstein" target="_blank">CaffeineMonkey</a> from the guys at SecureWorks, and so on and so forth (sorry if I left out anyone - I'm still working on 3 differemt time zones and keeping up with the "schedule" here...).

This means so much for us and really gives us a huge boost in terms of putting more research effort and working with our colleagues from other vendors.

In this optimistic tone, we are now facing DefCon - be sure to catch our <a href="https://www.defcon.org/html/defcon-15/dc-15-speakers.html#Raff" target="_blank">presentation</a> on Sunday - this stuff is really hot...
