Sometimes the only thing you can say about something boils down to the sound of your palm hitting your forehead. We have been seeing many ways in which criminals try to attack unsuspecting users and take over their PCs. One of which has been for quite some time the usage of advertisements as a vehicle to run malicious code on the victim's browser - also exploiting the fact that these ads show up on the most legitimate sites.

Recently, I ran across an article that "exposes" such a scheme as if it was completely new (see <a href="http://www.theregister.co.uk/2009/09/24/malware_ads_google_yahoo/">Register article here</a>). My initial response was to <a href="http://twitter.com/iiamit/status/4452804713">tweet about it</a> as it reminded me of how we covered the same issue some years ago. It was late and I was trying to recall how far back was it since this coverage, and surprisingly I got it right! 2007...

Having been running this blog which saves all of my "historical" posts, there is even one dating back to <a href="http://www.iamit.org/blog/2007/09/widgetsadvertisements/">September 2007 here</a>, which references a report I issued for the 2nd quarter of 2007 (means it was written in May) and tracks the story published on the Q1 report (which would mean that I almost missed it and some of these were tracked back at the end of 2006).
